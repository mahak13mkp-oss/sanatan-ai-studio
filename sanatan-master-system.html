PHASE 2 — Script Writing System
15-20 minute video = ~2500-3200 words. Narrator-only format. Cinematic storytelling.

Script Structure — 6-Act Formula

ActDurationWordsContentHook Level
1 — COLD OPEN0:00-0:60120-150Shock statement / mystery / unanswered question. NO intro, NO "hello doston". Straight to the drama.🔥🔥🔥
2 — SETUP0:60-3:00300-400Context: characters, setting, time period. Visual world-building. Original text reference (e.g., "Mahabharat ke Udhyog Parv mein likha hai...")🔥🔥
3 — RISING ACTION3:00-8:00600-800Build tension. Multiple layers. Each layer reveals more. Use pauses for effect. "Lekin kya aap jante hain...?"🔥🔥🔥
4 — TWIST / CLIMAX8:00-12:00600-800The BIG reveal. The hidden truth. What nobody tells you. Most emotional/cinematic part.🔥🔥🔥🔥
5 — MODERN CONNECTION12:00-15:00400-500Connect to today's life. Why this story matters NOW. Life lesson, philosophical depth.🔥🔥
6 — CLOSING + CTA15:00-16:00150-200Powerful final line. Next video teaser. "Agli video mein hum baat karenge..." + Subscribe CTA🔥🔥

Script Writing Rules (Non-Negotiable)

✅Narrator-Only: Sirf narrator ki awaaz. Koi character dialogue NHI. Agar koi character bol raha hai, to narrator bole: "Krishna ne Arjun se kaha — '...'"
✅Research-Based: Har claim ke saath source reference (e.g., "Vishnu Puran, Chapter 18")
✅Hindi + Sanskrit Mix: Main language Hindi. Sanskrit shabd/jaap/strotras original form mein.
✅Twist Mandatory: Har video mein kam se kam 1 hidden truth ya unexpected angle.
✅Open Loops: "Aur uske baad hua... ye hum baad mein jaanenge" → viewer ko end tak pakde rakhna.
✅Emotional Pacing: Har 2-3 minute mein ek naya emotional beat — fear, wonder, sadness, anger, hope.
✅Scene Descriptions: Script mein [VISUAL:] tags for AI image generation prompts.
❌NO "Hello doston", "Channel ko subscribe karo" at start. Hook first, CTA at end.
❌NO factual mistakes about mythology. Cross-verify from Puranas.
❌NO comparing religions. Pure Sanatan storytelling.
